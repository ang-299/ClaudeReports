Business model

Consilio is a global, tech-enabled legal services provider focused on eDiscovery: collecting, hosting, processing ("ingesting") and reviewing electronic data that becomes evidence in litigation, investigations and regulatory matters. Customers are ~90% corporates and ~10% law firms (blue chips ~50%, big tech ~25%, banks ~15%); no customer is above 10% of revenue and no single matter above 5%.

Revenue mix (approx.)

Hosting / eDiscovery 50% Review 30% Ent. Legal 20%
  • Hosting / eDiscovery: priced on data volume. Up to ~75% gross margin and the bulk of profit. Hosted data is bundled with processing and analytics, which makes it sticky.
  • Review solutions: contract attorneys billed hourly. Labor-heavy with mid-20s gross margins, ~15% of GP/EBITDA. First-pass review is the most automatable and lowest-margin piece.
  • Enterprise Legal Solutions: on-demand lawyers for non-core work, also hourly.

Why it earns its margins (analyst view)

  • Scale and bundled pricing: large clients get discounted hosting and law firms are steered to the vendor. ~60-70% of eDiscovery revenue comes from existing matters that run ~5+ years and cannot be migrated quickly.
  • Courts require key parts of discovery to be human-verified, which limits near-term AI substitution.

Ownership, capital structure and debate

  • Stone Point bought it in 2021 for ~$3bn (~9x EBITDA). Debt: $1.98bn Amendment No. 5 first-lien TL (S+375, May 2028), $80mm revolver (S+350), prior second lien retired Dec 2024.
  • Key risks: AI disrupting document review; Relativity (the dominant review platform) pushing clients to RelativityOne and competing with Consilio's own AI (Rel Server closed to new matters from 1 Jan 2028); Microsoft Purview and Google self-service filtering at source; price compression. The loan sold off ~15+pts on these fears.

Fundamentals (2Q26, reported 11 Aug)

  • Revenue +9% y/y, EBITDA +10% y/y at a 31% margin (30% in 2Q25). FY26 guide raised to revenue $1,195-1,240mm and Adj. EBITDA $360-375mm.
  • Net leverage 4.8x, $180mm cash, $275mm liquidity. KPIs: hosted data +18% y/y, ingested data +35% (lumpy), review hours +2%.
  • Docs (per analyst note): ~$1.5bn general incremental capacity, 7.5x net first-lien springing covenant tested only if the revolver is drawn above 35%. Next earnings expected November.

Recent diligence (Aug to Sep)

  • Expert call, 21 Sep (former Consilio PM): "the Walmart of eDiscovery" with scale-driven margins. Large clients get favorable bundled pricing; vendors discount hosting and recoup through services. AI-led review remains risky and unproven; per-document, per-iteration pricing is a barrier. Negative on the employer's cost discipline and utilisation culture.
  • Cleary eDiscovery catch-up, 4 Sep: continued price compression expected; vendors are shifting to consumption-based AI pricing. Hosting keeps growing with data volumes. Long-term risk is Microsoft or Google filtering at source and self-hosting. Relativity is hostile to Consilio's AI; existing matters can stay on Rel Server after 2028, so few forced migrations.
  • BofA conference lender feedback: Ares found management bullish and possibly "over-salesy", but confident AI risk is small and Relativity is not a problem.
  • Quick Pitch note (K. Rajput section): Relativity hosts ~30% of active data (not 15%); 87% of Consilio is on private cloud; a big buyer was reported accumulating the loan (see compliance note).
